# Privacy Policy — Amaranth Tweaks

Amaranth Tweaks runs only on the Okestro groupware website (https://gw.okestro.com).

## Data collected: none

The extension does not collect, store, or transmit any of your data to the developer or to any third party. It contains no analytics, no tracking, and no external servers.

## What it accesses, and why

- It reads and resizes the groupware's on-page popups locally in your browser, so tall content fits without scrolling.
- It reads your check-in time from the groupware's own API and shows it in the page header. That request goes only to gw.okestro.com — the site you are already signed in to — using your existing session.
- It extends the lifetime of the groupware's own login cookies in your browser so you are not logged out when you restart the browser.

Everything above happens locally in your browser or in direct communication with gw.okestro.com. No information leaves that first-party context.
